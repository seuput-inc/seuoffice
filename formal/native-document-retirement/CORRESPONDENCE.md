## Owner succession is not rebinding

`NativeRequestOwner::prepare_document_successor` stages a private non-Clone PreparedDocumentOwner before gates. It retains the same native value, authority, view, renderer, ingress/asset/media loss, and PDF leases/revision. Only the retired generic app-document subscription is replaced with the fresh staged lease. `AdmittedDocumentRetirement::renew_owner` exposes it only after retiring its exact captured predecessor, once per document, without subscription creation/current lookups under gates. A newly looked-up current owner or detached boolean approval is not a valid refinement.
